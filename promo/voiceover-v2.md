# Voiceover script, version 2 (about 50 seconds)

Record this yourself, or use it with a voice you choose. Speak warmly and a little slowly, like telling a friend about something useful. Leave a small pause at each timestamp. The picture is timed to these lines, so keep each one inside its window. `captions-v2.srt` has the same lines with times, for captions.

| Time | Line |
|---|---|
| 0:00 - 0:04 | People have questions. You have answers. |
| 0:04 - 0:07 | Now you can get paid for them. |
| 0:07 - 0:12 | Meet Maya. She's a career coach. |
| 0:13 - 0:18 | She sets her price, and chooses how fast she'll reply. |
| 0:19 - 0:23 | Then she shares one link, anywhere her audience is. |
| 0:24 - 0:32 | Someone asks her about two job offers. Their card is only held, and she's paid when she answers in time. |
| 0:33 - 0:40 | She replies in her own words, and the answer lands in their inbox. Their email stays private. |
| 0:40 - 0:45 | And if they want more, she can offer a private 15-minute conversation, right on Drop Me A Question. |
| 0:46 - 0:50 | Your experience is valuable. Make it count. dropmeaquestion.com |

## Adding it to the video

1. Record the voice on your phone (a quiet room, phone about a hand's width from your mouth) or in Clipchamp's Record & create.
2. In Clipchamp, drop the silent video on the timeline and the voice on the audio track below it. Nudge the voice so each line starts at its time.
3. Add quiet music under it, at about 10 to 15 percent volume.
4. Turn on captions (Clipchamp can import `captions-v2.srt` or make captions from the voice) and export at 1080p.

If you use a HeyGen voice instead, paste the script in, pick a natural, unhurried voice, and check the lines end before the next scene begins.
